Biography

As a kid, I wanted to be a neurosurgeon, teacher, scientist at the same time. But, also, the brain, as the most complex system, inspires me. So, I want to become an Artificial Intelligence Engineer. Sounds funny, right? I think AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) goes through the intersection of Natural Language Processing and Computer vision with lending some tricks from Reinforcement Learning.

My research focuses on applying Machine Learning methods to find insights into bacterial genes.

I love to be better 1% at a time and be more productive every day. I have a taste for Philosophy and Psychology, and Personal Development.
